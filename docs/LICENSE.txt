IMPORTANT — DATA LOSS
---------------------
YSM stores your messages and encryption keys encrypted on this device only.
There is no server, no cloud backup and no recovery service. If you lose your
password, your authenticator app AND your recovery codes, your messages are
permanently unrecoverable. This is a consequence of the security design, not a
defect.
