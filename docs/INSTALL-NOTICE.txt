YSM — what this application does and does not protect
=====================================================

BEFORE YOU INSTALL
------------------

YSM is an end-to-end encrypted messenger. Read this short notice; it states the
limits honestly so you are not surprised later.

WHAT YSM PROTECTS
-----------------
- Your messages and files are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM and
  signed with Ed25519. Nobody without your key can read or alter them.
- Your database on disk is encrypted. Someone who copies the file cannot read
  your messages, contacts or keys.
- There is no server. Nothing you type is ever sent anywhere by the application.
  No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no update checks.

HOW MESSAGES TRAVEL
-------------------
YSM does not include a network service. To send a message you export a small
encrypted file and deliver it yourself (email, USB drive, your own relay). The
recipient imports it. Whoever carries the file sees only ciphertext, but they
can see *that* a file was sent, its approximate size, and when.

WHAT YSM DOES NOT PROTECT
-------------------------
- A compromised computer. If malware can read this app's memory while unlocked,
  it can read your messages.
- Someone forcing you to reveal your password.
- Your choice of transport. If you send files by email, the email provider learns
  who is talking to whom.
- Past messages if your device key is later stolen. YSM v1 does not yet rotate
  keys per message (no forward secrecy).

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES
---------------------
1. Compare the "safety number" with your contact in person or by voice call.
   This is the only way to be sure nobody is intercepting your conversation.
   The app shows it prominently but cannot verify it for you.
2. Save your recovery codes offline. If you lose your password, your
   authenticator app AND your recovery codes, your data is unrecoverable.
3. Keep your password long. It is the only thing protecting your local vault.

TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION IS REQUIRED
-------------------------------------
YSM requires an authenticator code every time you unlock. Have an authenticator
app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Aegis, or any TOTP app) ready before you create
an account.

LANGUAGES
---------
The application interface is available in English, Dutch and Farsi.
